# The content signals and their meanings are:

# not the prose, so being indexed, quoted in AI answers and present in model knowledge is the
# goal rather than something to defend against.
#

#   Claude. Setting it to "no" would ask those engines NOT to use this site in their answers.
#
# To reserve training rights while keeping full search + AI-answer visibility, change every
# "ai-train=yes" below to "ai-train=no" AND switch the training crawlers near the bottom of this
# file (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, cohere-ai, Amazonbot, Bytespider, meta-externalagent) from
# "Allow: /" to "Disallow: /", so the two mechanisms do not contradict each other.
#
# Note: a robots.txt expresses preferences. It does not technically prevent a crawler from
# taking content, and not all operators honour it.
#
# Signals are repeated per group on purpose: under RFC 9309 a crawler obeys only the single most
# specific group matching its name, so an agent with its own group below would never read a
# signal that appeared only under "User-agent: *".
